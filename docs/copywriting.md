# Copywriting Guide

Write like the lead sales copywriter for a premium Lagos cleaning company. The reader is busy, has been let down by cleaners before, and wants to know one thing: will this crew get it right the first time?

## Voice

- Confident, calm, precise. Premium, not flashy.
- Speak to outcomes: move-in ready, handover ready, dust-free, inspection passed, zero stress.
- Specific beats generic. "Paint splatter, cement haze and sawdust removed from every surface, vent and window track" beats "We clean everything".
- Short sentences. Active voice. Second person ("your home", "your site").
- British/Nigerian English spelling: colour, organise, centre, programme.
- Lagos is home. Local references are welcome where they fit naturally (estates, handovers, dust season, harmattan).

## Audiences

1. **Homeowners and tenants:** moving in or out, preparing for guests, overdue deep cleans, recurring upkeep.
2. **Developers, contractors, estate and property managers:** post-construction handovers, snag-free finishing, facility maintenance.
3. **Offices, schools, clinics and businesses:** fogging and disinfection, facility maintenance.

## Conversion patterns

- Every section earns the next scroll or a click. End sections with a clear action.
- Button labels are verbs with outcomes: "Get a Free Quote", "Chat With Us on WhatsApp", "Book Your Deep Clean", "Call Now". Never "Submit" or "Click Here".
- Pair each button with a reassurance line where helpful: "Reply within opening hours, Monday to Saturday."
- Build trust only with verified facts (docs/company.md). Never invent numbers, reviews or certifications.
- Quotes, not prices: "Every space is different. Tell us about yours and we will send a clear, fixed quote."

## Mechanics (from docs/rules.md, repeated because they matter)

- No emojis, no em or en dashes, no text arrows.
- Title Case for headings and buttons. Sentence case for body copy.
- Numbers: "4-bedroom", "08:00 to 18:00", "RC 7312822G".
- Alt text describes the scene: "Freshly cleaned open-plan living room in a new Lekki duplex after post-construction cleaning".

## Examples of the bar

- Weak: "We offer quality cleaning services."
- Strong: "Builders leave. We make it liveable. Post-construction cleaning that takes your property from dusty shell to handover ready."

- Weak: "Contact us today."
- Strong: "Tell us about your space. We will reply on WhatsApp with a clear quote, no site visit needed for most homes."
  (Only use claims like "no site visit needed" after the client confirms them. Otherwise keep it to what is verified.)
